How AC Targets Are Calculated

Once the B Plan is locked, individual AC targets are computed using a formula that pulls from the Slabs sheet via XLOOKUP.

Monthly Revenue Target — Formula Logic
Monthly Target (USD) =
  Program ARPU × Maturity Payment Rate × Grade Multiplier
The Slabs sheet stores lookup tables for all three variables. The AC Targets sheet uses XLOOKUP to auto-populate targets based on each AC’s date of joining, grade, and assigned program group.
The Three Variables
Variable 1

Maturity (M0 → M24+)

Based on weeks since the AC’s date of joining. Starts at M0 (lowest rate) and ramps up each month. By M12 the multiplier is ~2.4x, by M24+ ~2.45x — rewarding tenure and productivity growth.

Variable 2

Grade Multiplier (G0–G3)

Each AC has a designated grade. G0 = 0.8x (new/junior), G1 = 1.0x (standard), G2 = 1.05x, G3 = 1.06x. Senior grades carry a slightly higher target expectation.

Variable 3

Program ARPU

Each program group has a defined Average Revenue Per Unit (enrolment). E.g., GGU/EU = ~$17,500; GGU GenAI = ~$19,000; NP_EMEA = ~$14,000; GGU US = ~$25,000. Higher-value programs carry higher ARPUs.

Monthly → Weekly Breakdown

Monthly targets are split into weekly targets across the 3-month quarter (12 weeks). The formula accounts for the AC’s maturity changing month-on-month — so their Week 1–4 target may differ from Week 9–12 as they ramp up.

Target Roll-Up Hierarchy
LevelHow Calculated
AC LevelProgram ARPU × Maturity Rate × Grade Multiplier × weeks in month
Manager LevelSum of all AC targets under the Manager
SM LevelSum of all Manager targets under the SM
📐Floor targets are approximately ~25% higher than the B Plan target. This buffer ensures that even if the team achieves 80–85% of the floor, the B Plan is still met. Buffer varies quarter to quarter.

Effective 1st January 2026. ROW team only. Monthly cycle driven by BCT data from MIS FinOps.

Calculation — Step by Step
Step 1 — Enrollment-Level Incentive
Enrollment Incentive =
  Gross Collected Revenue (per enrollment)
  × Applicable Slab % (based on AC’s total achievement %)
  × Balance Collection Rider (0.5x / 1.0x / 1.2x)
Calculated individually for each enrollment. Domestic revenue counts at 50% of face value for both slab qualification and payout.
Step 2 — Roll Up to AC Level
AC Monthly Incentive =
  Σ All Enrollment Incentives + Referral Incentive
  × Referral Rider (1.0 if met | 0.75 if not met)
The Referral Rider applies to the entire AC payout — if the AC has no referral enrolment or leads that month, their total incentive is cut by 25%.

Tracker Structure — 5 Sheets
SheetPurpose
OverallsHigh-level summary of total given, collected, dropouts, overdue, and future collections — split across Monthly PAYG vs Non-Monthly PAYG, for ROW and VN teams.

StatusWhat It Means

4. Overdue (Dropout)Learner is overdue AND has dropped out. Outstanding balance remains.

PAYG Types
Monthly PAYG

Monthly Installments

Learner pays monthly. Installment = Total Fee ÷ Number of Installments. Tracked month-on-month in the Summary sheet.

Non-Monthly PAYG

Custom Schedule

Learner pays on a non-monthly schedule (e.g., 0/4/8 split or custom milestones). Tracked separately in the Overalls sheet.
